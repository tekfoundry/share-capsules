# Code Of Conduct

Share Capsules uses the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1, as the baseline for community behavior.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good faith while staying precise about risks and evidence.
- Welcome newcomers and independent implementers.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas, code, documentation, and security properties.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Using project spaces to pressure people into unsafe disclosure of credentials, identity material, private content, or vulnerability details.
- Disruptive behavior that makes review or collaboration materially harder.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact TekFoundry at [info@tekfoundry.com](mailto:info@tekfoundry.com).

For security-sensitive conduct or vulnerability matters, use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

TekFoundry may remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or take other moderation action when needed to keep the project reviewable and safe.
